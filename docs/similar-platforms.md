# Landscape: platforms similar to Y

Research conducted August 2026 (multi-agent web sweep across five categories +
completeness pass). Bottom line: **no existing product combines Y's four
pillars** (public voting + opinion-vs-government gap + corporate transparency +
AI deliberation). Every pillar individually has mature incumbents; the
integration is the unclaimed territory. Details below, grouped by category.

Legend for overlap: R = Referendum, D = Discrepancy, T = Transparency, DE = Deliberate.

## Closest overall matches (ranked)

| # | Platform | Pillars | Status | What it lacks vs Y |
|---|----------|---------|--------|--------------------|
| 1 | **Countable / Causes** (causes.com) | R + D + weak DE | Effectively defunct (peaked 2017–19, degraded after 2020 merger) | Transparency layer, AI facilitation, thresholds, global scope |
| 2 | **MiVote** (Australia) | DE + R, same "no alignment" claim | Defunct (~2016–2020) | Discrepancy, transparency; never elected anyone |
| 3 | **abgeordnetenwatch.de** | D + T (MP votes beside their donations/side income/lobby ties) | Active since 2004 | Public voting, AI deliberation; Germany-only |
| 4 | **Polis / vTaiwan + Join.gov.tw** | R-adjacent + DE + procedural D | Active (Taiwan, national scale) | Rejects binary ballots/supermajority; government-convened; no transparency DB |
| 5 | **Liquid.us** | R + D fused exactly as Y intends (vote on bills, grade legislators against constituents) | Dormant, tiny, US-only | Transparency, AI deliberation, scale |

## 1. E-democracy / civic voting platforms

- **Decidim** (decidim.org) — R/DE/partial T. Active. Open-source, 450+ deployments, ~30 countries (Barcelona, EU Conference on the Future of Europe, NYC PB). "No owner" association-governed ethos closest to Y's.
- **CONSUL Democracy** (consuldemocracy.org) — R (threshold-triggers-action mechanic at city scale). Active, 250+ cities, 35 countries. UN Public Service Award 2018.
- **Your Priorities / Citizens Foundation** (citizens.is) — R/DE (forced pro-vs-con argument structure). Active, 50+ countries; Better Reykjavik since 2010. #1 in People Powered 2025 ratings.
- **Loomio** (loomio.com) — DE/weak R, group-internal. Active worker co-op since 2012.
- **Go Vocal, ex-CitizenLab** (govocal.com) — R/partial DE (AI sensemaking for officials). Active, ~500 governments.
- **Ethelo** (ethelo.com) — R/DE; the only platform explicitly optimizing for supermajority-style broad support (philosophically near Y's 75% bar). Active; Engaged California, Canadian municipalities.
- **Join platform, Taiwan** (join.gov.tw) — R/partial D: 5,000 signatures compel a published point-by-point government response. ~⅓ of Taiwan's population has used it.
- **DemocracyOS / Democracy Earth "Sovereign"** — conceptually the closest predecessor of Y's pillar 1 (borderless, ownerless voting). Dormant/pivoted to crypto. Cautionary.
- **LiquidFeedback** (liquidfeedback.com) — liquid democracy for parties (Pirates, Five Star adjacents). Active since 2009.
- **Rousseau (Five Star Movement)** — ~100k members' online votes decided Italy's 2019 coalition; killed by opacity scandals 2021. The stress test of app votes mattering.
- **adhocracy+** (adhocracy.plus) — ~280 German municipalities incl. meinBerlin. Active.
- **Citizen OS / Rahvaalgatus.ee** — Estonian initiative portal: 1,000 verified signatures legally oblige parliament to respond. Active.
- **European Citizens' Initiative** (citizens-initiative.europa.eu) — the only legally institutionalized transnational threshold mechanism (1M signatures, 7 states). Active since 2012.
- **Mudamos** (Brazil) — cryptographically verified signatures for constitutional popular initiatives. Active (maintenance).
- **Change.org** — 500M+ users, 196 countries; the largest raw "public demand" channel, but petitions ≠ votes. Active.
- **Vocdoni** (vocdoni.io) — censorship-resistant, verifiable voting protocol (born of the 2017 Catalan referendum); the most literal "no owner" voting infrastructure. Active.
- **Snapshot** (snapshot.box) — ownerless live-tallied voting at scale, but token-weighted DAO governance. Active.
- **Voatz** — blockchain mobile voting for real US elections; MIT security teardown ended government use. The security cautionary tale.

## 2. Opinion-vs-government gap trackers (Y's most distinctive pillar)

- **TheyWorkForYou + Votes** (mySociety, UK) — the government-action half of the gap, since 2004; Votes sub-site added 2025.
- **GovTrack.us** — every US roll-call since 1789; one-person-led, nonpartisan. Active.
- **abgeordnetenwatch.de** — see closest matches. The only D+T fusion in production.
- **VoteWatch Europe** — EU vote analytics 2009–2022; **died of funding starvation** (fragility warning).
- **HowTheyVote.eu** — open-source, grant-funded successor. Active.
- **They Vote For You** (OpenAustralia) — plain-English MP voting positions. Active.
- **smartvote + smartmonitor** (Switzerland) — formally closes the loop: candidates' pre-election questionnaire answers vs their actual parliamentary votes. Active since 2003.
- **Polimeter** (Université Laval) — expert-coded promise-kept/broken tracking (578 promises for one Trudeau mandate). Active.
- **Voteview** (UCLA) — DW-NOMINATE ideological measurement of every US Congress vote. Active.
- **iSideWith** — 53M+ users, 41 countries, 2.7B issue answers; matches public answers to politicians' stated positions. The largest standing informal ballot dataset. Active.
- **Vote Smart** (justfacts.votesmart.org) — stated positions vs record for ~40k US officials since 1992. Active.
- **Gilens & Page 2014 dataset** — the academic proof of Y's thesis: 1,779 opinion-vs-policy cases showing near-zero responsiveness to average citizens. Not a platform — the pipeline Y would need to run continuously.

## 3. Transparency / money-in-politics databases

- **OpenSecrets** (opensecrets.org) — the flagship; federal + 50 states after the 2021 FollowTheMoney merger. Links money to issue areas. Active.
- **LittleSis** (littlesis.org) — the closest analogue to Y's connections graph ("involuntary facebook of the powerful", Oligrapher power maps). Activist-aligned, unlike Y's neutrality claim. Active.
- **OpenCorporates** — 230M+ companies, 140+ jurisdictions; upstream data source, not a competitor. Active.
- **Open Ownership** — beneficial-ownership standards + transnational register; 104 countries now have live registers. Active.
- **LobbyFacts.eu** — EU lobby spending rankings back to 2012. Active.
- **Integrity Watch** (Transparency International EU + national editions) — lobby meetings, MEP side jobs, conflict-of-interest visualization. Active.
- **OCCRP Aleph** — 1B+ records, 180+ countries; investigator's toolkit, not citizen-facing. Active.
- **InfluenceMap / LobbyMap** — the strongest existing issue-linked influence tracker: scores 1,000+ companies' climate lobbying vs their public claims. Structurally the closest thing to Y's T+D combination, for one issue domain. Active.
- **MapLight** — historically the closest to Y's issue-linkage idea: org positions + money mapped to specific bills and votes (2007–2021). **Dataset discontinued/archived.**
- **Lobbywatch.ch** — all Swiss MPs' interest ties and lobbyist badges. Active.
- **ICIJ Offshore Leaks** — 810k+ offshore entities from Panama/Paradise/Pandora Papers; the hidden-ownership chains official registers miss. Active.
- **Violation Tracker** (Good Jobs First) — 700k+ US corporate misconduct cases rolled up to ~3,000 parents; UK + Global editions. Active.

## 4. AI-facilitated deliberation

- **Polis** (pol.is) — the field's backbone; 10M+ participants; behind vTaiwan and Anthropic's Collective Constitutional AI. Active.
- **Talk to the City** (AI Objectives Institute) — LLM clusters public input into browsable perspective reports you can "interview". Active since 2023.
- **Habermas Machine** (Google DeepMind, *Science* Oct 2024) — LLM mediator whose common-ground statements beat human mediators with 5,734 UK participants. **The scientific validation of Y's neutral-AI-facilitator concept.** Research only.
- **Stanford Online Deliberation Platform** — automated moderator, 100k+ deliberation hours, 35+ countries. Active.
- **Deliberation.io** (Stanford + MIT, 2025) — LLM Socratic dialogue + opinion mapping; D.C. government pilot. The newest direct DE analogue. Active.
- **Make.org** — millions of participants in EU consultations; Panoramic AI explains topics before participation; Democratic Commons research program. Active.
- **Jigsaw Sensemaker** (Google) — LLM sensemaking over civic conversations (Bowling Green ~8k participants; "We The People" 2,400 representative Americans). Active.
- **Anthropic + CIP: Collective Constitutional AI** (2023) — ~1,000 Americans voted principles into Claude's constitution via Polis. One-off landmark.
- **Global Dialogues** (CIP, globaldialogues.ai) — recurring AI-facilitated global deliberation, ~70 countries, bimonthly. The closest operating "global public voice" infrastructure. Active since 2024.
- **Cortico / Fora** (MIT CCC) — AI-assisted analysis of recorded community conversations. Active.
- **Engaged California** (engaged.ca.gov) — first US state deliberation platform (2025); government-owned, inverting Y's audit-the-government premise. Active.
- **Remesh** — commercial AI dialogues (UN peacebuilding in Yemen/Libya/Iraq). Active.
- **Kialo** — structured pro/con argument trees, 1M+ users; the non-AI ancestor of Y's DE pillar. Active.
- **AI Steve** (UK 2024 election) — AI candidate avatar + human proxy pledged to vote as AI-aggregated constituents direct. ~179 votes. The most literal R+DE embryo.

## 5. Global referendum / world assembly projects

- **Global Assembly** (globalassembly.org, COP26) — first global citizens' assembly: 100 members by civic lottery representing humanity; People's Declaration at COP26. Dormant since.
- **Iswe Foundation Global Citizens' Assembly** (iswe.org) — permanent successor: launched at UN Summit of the Future 2024, docked with COP30; target 10M participants by 2030. **The best-funded live effort in Y's ambition space** — but sortition + deliberation, not open voting.
- **The Global Vote** (Simon Anholt) — symbolic worldwide votes in other countries' elections since 2016; 146 countries; semi-dormant.
- **Vote World Parliament** — standing online global referendum since ~2007: **~22,000 votes in 15 years** (95.3% yes). The starkest reach-vs-ambition cautionary number in this file.
- **Democracy Without Borders / UN World Citizens' Initiative** — 300+ org campaign for a formal 5M-signature UN agenda mechanism; still a proposal.
- **UN MY World 2015** — ~10M votes from 194 countries, the largest global issue vote ever; majority collected **offline on paper**. Concluded 2015.
- **UNICEF U-Report** (ureport.in) — 20–28M young people, 90+ countries, recurring SMS/WhatsApp polls with live public dashboards. The largest live global issue-polling infrastructure; proof low-bandwidth channels matter.
- **World Wide Views** (Danish Board of Technology) — same-day deliberate-then-vote in up to 76 countries with identical briefing materials, results compared against UN negotiating positions (closest precedent for Y's deliberate→vote→gap loop). Dormant since 2015.
- **UNDP Mission 1.5 / Peoples' Climate Vote** — 1.2M respondents, 50 countries; game educates on trade-offs before voting, results handed to governments. Recurring survey.
- **The World Parliament Experiment** (world-parliament.org, since 1999) — open global voting simulation; hundreds-to-thousands of users over 25 years.
- **Global Democracy** (globaldemocracy.com) — "vote for ideas not people," ~14k users, dormant.
- **referendum.world** — the long tail: micro-scale one-person global-referendum sites.

### Adjacent: aggregate-judgment platforms

- **Metaculus** — calibrated public forecasting used by CDC/WHO. Active.
- **Polymarket** — market-priced public sentiment ($3.2B on the 2024 US election). Active.
- **Simpol** (simpol.org) — citizens pledge votes to candidates who commit to simultaneous global policy; 100+ UK MPs signed. Active since 2000.

## Strategic takeaways for Y

1. **The integration is unclaimed.** Nobody has wired a corporate-power database
   into a voting product, and nobody runs a standing global issue ballot with a
   supermajority threshold. Every component exists; the combination doesn't.
2. **The graveyard is instructive.** Democracy Earth, Vote World Parliament,
   Global Democracy, MiVote, Countable, TWPE all shared parts of Y's vision and
   died of the same two diseases: no distribution (nobody arrives) and no
   consequence (nothing happens when you vote). Taiwan's Join and Estonia's
   Rahvaalgatus survived because a threshold *legally obliges a response*.
   Y's ¾ threshold needs an answer to "then what?"
3. **The gap pillar has a proven method.** Gilens & Page (and Polimeter's
   pledge-coding) show how to do opinion-vs-action rigorously; InfluenceMap
   shows issue-linked influence scoring works and moves markets.
4. **The AI facilitator is newly validated.** DeepMind's Habermas Machine
   (Science, 2024) showed an LLM mediator can beat human mediators at finding
   common ground — Y's Deliberate pillar is riding a real research wave
   (Deliberation.io, Global Dialogues, Engaged California all launched 2024–26).
5. **Global reach requires low bandwidth.** The only things that reached tens of
   millions globally (U-Report, MY World) did it via SMS/WhatsApp/paper, not a
   React web app. Y's stated voice-first accessibility goal points the same way.
