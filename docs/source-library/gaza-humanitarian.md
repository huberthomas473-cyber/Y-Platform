# Gaza: Sanctions Until Ceasefire & Access — verification log

**Ballot text:** "Should the international community impose sanctions until there is a ceasefire and full humanitarian access in Gaza?"

## ⚠️ Wording status: FLAGGED — retire and replace (rulebook §4)

The question's premise has been overtaken by events: a ceasefire (fragile, contested,
but real) has existed since 10 Oct 2025. "Sanctions **until there is a ceasefire**" is
no longer a coherent live proposition. Per rulebook §4, live questions are never edited
in place — recommendation: **retire this issue** (results stay visible, marked closed)
and open a successor with fresh wording, e.g. conditioning on *implementation* (full
humanitarian access, protection of civilians) rather than on reaching a ceasefire.
Successor wording needs the §3 two-person review before shipping.
