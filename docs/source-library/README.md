**the claim → the source that supports it → verification status → date → checker.**
The rules live in [../editorial-rulebook.md](../editorial-rulebook.md). A claim that
loses its source gets deleted from the app, not kept.

## Standing research databases

The heavy lifting for money-trail research is already done by free public databases
(see also [../similar-platforms.md](../similar-platforms.md)):

- **OpenSecrets** — opensecrets.org — US federal + state lobbying, PACs, revolving door
- **OpenCorporates** — opencorporates.com — company registries, 140+ jurisdictions
- **LittleSis** — littlesis.org — relationship maps of executives, boards, donors
- **ICIJ Offshore Leaks** — offshoreleaks.icij.org — offshore entities from the Panama/Paradise/Pandora Papers
- **Business & Human Rights Resource Centre** — business-humanrights.org — company-by-company allegation tracking with company responses
- **AFSC Investigate** — investigate.afsc.org — screening research on companies in conflict/occupation contexts
- **UN documents** — undocs.org (resolutions by symbol, e.g. S/RES/2803)

## What "OPEN" means

Gaps are marked **OPEN** rather than papered over. An OPEN item is a research task
for a volunteer, in priority order: (1) public-opinion data per issue, (2) additional
government positions beyond the single seeded actor, (3) transparency rows for
actors not yet covered.
