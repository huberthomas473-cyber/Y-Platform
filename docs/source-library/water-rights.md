# Water as an Enforceable Right — verification log

**Ballot text:** "Should access to clean drinking water be a legally enforceable human right in every country?"
**Wording status:** passes rulebook §3 ("legally enforceable" is the mechanism; a "no" can coherently prefer non-justiciable policy routes).

| The right is not enforceable in most national courts | Res 64/292 is non-binding; constitutional enforceability exists only in a minority of states (e.g. South Africa, Slovenia). Attribution phrased carefully ("not enforceable in most national courts") | ✅ Verified (framing) | 7 Aug 2026 |

| Stance: `partial` | Recognition without enforceability | ✅ Editorial judgment | 7 Aug 2026 |
