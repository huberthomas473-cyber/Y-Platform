# Beach Access in Jamaica — verification log

**Ballot text:** "Should free public access to Jamaica's beaches be guaranteed in law for all citizens?"
**Wording status:** passes rulebook §3.

## Claims shown in the app

| Claim | Source | Status | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beach Control Act (1956) vests the foreshore in the Crown, licensed to operators | The Beach Control Act, Laws of Jamaica — laws.moj.gov.jm/library/statute/the-beach-control-act (link live) | ✅ Verified | 7 Aug 2026 |
| No general legal right of free beach access exists | Same Act (access via licences); confirmed by government's own policy rationale ("the 1956 Act… did not adequately address beach access and will have to be replaced" — JIS/MEGID) | ✅ Verified | 7 Aug 2026 |
| Draft Beach Access and Management Policy went to Cabinet 2025, tabled March 2026 | MEGID (megid.gov.jm, BAMP Green Paper PDF); Jamaica Observer, 20 Mar 2026 (PM Holness budget-debate announcement) | ✅ Verified | 7 Aug 2026 |
| Campaigners say the framework entrenches restrictions | Jamaica Gleaner, "Beach policy under fire", 27 Mar 2026; JaBBEM (jabbem.org) position | ✅ Verified | 7 Aug 2026 |
| No enforceable guarantee enacted as of Aug 2026 | Absence of enacted legislation; BAMP is a green paper (policy, not law) | ✅ Verified | 7 Aug 2026 |
| Stance: `partial` (was `opposed` pre-2026) | Policy motion exists but campaigners dispute direction; judgment call logged here | ✅ Editorial judgment | 7 Aug 2026 |
