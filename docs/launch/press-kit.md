# Press kit — Y × Beach Access in Jamaica

*One page for journalists. Written before you asked. Last updated 7 Aug 2026.*

## What Y is

Y is a voluntary global assembly: an open platform where people vote on real issues,
deliberate with a strictly neutral AI facilitator before voting, and see the gap
between what people want and what institutions do. It has **no owner, no algorithm,
and no political alignment**. The code is open source, and every factual claim on the
platform carries a checkable source under a published [editorial rulebook](../editorial-rulebook.md).

## The launch question

> **"Should free public access to Jamaica's beaches be guaranteed in law for all citizens?"**

Why this question: it is a live national debate. Under the Beach Control Act (1956)
the foreshore is Crown property licensed to operators; a draft Beach Access and
Management Policy was tabled in March 2026 and campaigners (JaBBEM, Jamaica
Environment Trust) argue it entrenches restrictions rather than removing them.
Every claim in that sentence is sourced in our public
[research file](../source-library/beach-access.md).

## How the number works — and its limits

- One account = one confirmed email = at most one vote per issue; votes can be
  changed, only the latest counts. Votes are written by a rate-limited server, never
  directly by browsers. Results shown are aggregates only.
- The consensus bar is **75% support among decided votes** (abstentions shown but
  not counted as decided), with a minimum of **1,000 decided votes** before any
  consequence fires.
- **The limits, plainly:** participation is self-selected and email addresses are
  not people — one person with several addresses can vote more than once. We rate
  limit and monitor, but treat Y's tally as an **indicative signal of organized
  public demand, not a representative poll** of Jamaicans. We will correct anyone —
  including our partners — who quotes it as a poll.

## The consequence (the part that's new)

Y's numbers come with a standing public commitment, [the 75% Rule](the-75-percent-rule.md):
at ≥75% support with ≥1,000 decided votes, Y formally delivers the result and its
evidence dossier to the Government of Jamaica (the ministry responsible for the
beach policy, and NEPA/NRCA), copied to the Opposition — then publishes their
response **or their documented silence** within 30 days. Both outcomes are permanent
public record.
