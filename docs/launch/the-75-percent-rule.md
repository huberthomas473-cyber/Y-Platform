# The 75% Rule — Y's standing commitment (v1.0)

*Published 7 Aug 2026. Applies to the launch issue: **Beach Access in Jamaica**
("Should free public access to Jamaica's beaches be guaranteed in law for all citizens?").
This page is the commitment the ballot links to. Changes are allowed only before the
trigger fires, and every change is publicly logged at the bottom.*

## What happens at 75%

**Trigger.** Both conditions, measured by the live public tally:

1. **≥ 75% support** among decided votes (yes ÷ (yes + no)), and
2. **≥ 1,000 decided votes** — a floor so that a handful of votes can never
   trigger a national claim.

**Action — within 14 days of the trigger:** Y formally delivers the result and its
**evidence dossier** (vote totals with methodology and stated limits, plus the
source-verified research file for the issue) to:

- the **Minister responsible for the Ministry of Economic Growth and Infrastructure
  Development** (the ministry that authored the Beach Access and Management Policy), and
- the **National Environment and Planning Agency / NRCA** (administrators of the
  Beach Control Act licensing regime),

copied to the **Office of the Leader of the Opposition**, and to the civil-society
campaigners on this issue (JaBBEM, Jamaica Environment Trust) for their records.
Delivery is by email **and** registered post, and the full delivery package is
published on Y the same day.

**Response window — 30 days.** Whatever comes back is published verbatim on a
public **Response page**. If nothing comes back, the Response page documents the
silence: what was sent, to whom, when, with what proof of delivery. Both outcomes
are permanent public record. *(This publish-the-silence mechanism is borrowed
deliberately from abgeordnetenwatch.de, which built real accountability power
with it in Germany.)*

## What Y will and will not claim

- The delivered number is presented **with its limits attached** — participation is
  self-selected, one account = one confirmed email (email accounts are not people),
  and the tally is an **indicative signal of organized public demand, not a
  representative poll** of Jamaicans. The dossier says this on page one.
- Y publishes the result **whatever it is** — including if support never reaches
  75%, or if "no" wins. Partners mobilize participation; nobody, including
  partners, gets editorial control over the question or the number.
- The count that matters is the count at a **pre-announced snapshot date** agreed
  and published when the campaign begins, so the goalposts cannot move mid-vote.

## Why this exists

A vote with no consequence is a poll nobody needs. This rule makes the consequence
mechanical and impossible to quietly drop: a threshold, a deadline, a named
recipient, and a public record of what they did — or didn't — say back.
