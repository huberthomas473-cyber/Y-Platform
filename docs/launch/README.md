# Launch plan — Beach Access in Jamaica

*Phase 3 of the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md): don't launch globally — launch on ONE
issue where Y can win. This folder is the working pack for that launch.*

## The strategy in one paragraph

Beach access is a real, live national debate in Jamaica with organized constituencies
on our exact question (JaBBEM's constitutional campaign, JET's Big Up Wi Beach,
fisherfolk with statutory stakes in the new policy). Y offers what no petition or
hashtag does: a **neutral, countable, source-verified number with a mechanical
consequence attached** — at 75% the result goes formally to the Government, and
their response or silence becomes public record. One number quoted in the Gleaner
or in Gordon House is worth more than six issues nobody sees.

## What only humans can do (in order)

1. **Name the campaign lead.** The roadmap is explicit: this workstream needs a
   leader more than the code does. Everything below is theirs.
2. **First conversation with JaBBEM** (then JET, then the Fishermen Co-operative
   Union) — the partner map has the ask, the ground rules, and the sequencing logic.
3. **Set the snapshot date** with the first committed partners, publish it in the
   75% Rule change log, and only then go to press.
4. **Fill the press-kit contact slot** and send it to the journalists already
   covering the BAMP fight (the Gleaner and Observer bylines in the research file
   are the warm list).

## Prerequisite

Phase 1 (email sign-in + server-guarded voting) must be tested and its database
migration applied **before** any partner brings members to vote — the press kit's
"how the number works" section describes that system and must be true on launch day.
