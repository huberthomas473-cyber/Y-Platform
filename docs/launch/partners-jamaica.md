# Partner map — Beach Access in Jamaica

*"Partner, don't broadcast": every platform that ever reached scale was carried by
organizations that already had members. This is the researched contact list for the
launch issue — real organizations, verified 7 Aug 2026. Contacting them is human
work; each row needs an owner.*

**The ask, in one sentence:** *"Bring your members to cast a formal, countable vote —
at 75% (min. 1,000 decided votes) the result and evidence dossier go formally to the
Government, and their response — or silence — becomes public record."*
[Full commitment →](the-75-percent-rule.md)

**Ground rule (from the editorial rulebook):** partners mobilize participation; they
get no editorial control over the question's wording or the published number. Y
publishes the result even if it's inconvenient. Say this in the first conversation —
it is why the number will be quotable.

## Sequencing

1. **JaBBEM first.** If the campaign closest to the question won't bring members, the
   launch premise is wrong — learn that before anything else.
2. JET second (credibility + legal framing for the dossier), fisherfolk third
   (constituency breadth), Tier 3 once there's a date.
3. Only after two Tier-1/2 partners commit: set the public **snapshot date** (see the
   75% Rule) and go to press with the kit.

## What partners get (the honest pitch)

- A neutral, countable, source-verified instrument their members can act on in
  two minutes — with a mechanical consequence attached.
- The evidence dossier, free to use in their own advocacy and court communications.
- What they don't get: control of the wording or the number. That independence is
  what makes the number worth quoting.
